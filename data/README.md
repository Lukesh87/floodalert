# Coimbatore Urban Flood Data & Hydrological Knowledge Base

This directory contains verified station metadata, historical flood incident records, and rainfall datasets for Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

## Geographic Context
- **Coordinates**: 11.0168° N, 76.9558° E
- **Average Elevation**: 411m to 440m MSL
- **Major River Basin**: Noyyal River (Cauvery basin tributary)
- **Key Water Bodies (System Tanks)**: Ukkadam Periyakulam, Singanallur, Valankulam, Kurichi, Selvampathy, Krishnampathi.
- **Critical Underpasses & Low Points**: Lanka Corner, Kikani underpass, Avinashi Road underpass.

## Files
1. `coimbatore_stations.json`: 10 micro-catchment monitoring stations with elevation, slope, imperviousness, and drainage ratings.
2. `coimbatore_historical_floods.csv`: Verified historical flood incidents with recorded pre-incident rainfall, affected landmarks, severity, and official verification status.
3. `coimbatore_rainfall_records.csv`: Curated rainfall timeseries representing typical wet, moderate, and extreme monsoon precipitation patterns.
